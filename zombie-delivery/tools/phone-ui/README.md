# Phone UI recorder

Run from `zombie-delivery/`: `python3 tools/phone-ui/check.py /path/to/luau`.

Copies the actual Phone, PhoneContract, PhoneLayout, PhoneRoute, PhonePopup, PhoneApps, PhoneMotion, OrdersUi, TasksApp, StyleApp and shared data into a temporary directory. The fixture supplies narrow service/instance stand-ins, then exercises real construction, server payload updates, action callbacks, resize and lifecycle cleanup. 6.0.1: every app opens in the centred popup and closes back to home (✕, backdrop, Q / ESC / B), and the phone never turns. 6.5: home is six big tiles in order (JOBS, MAP, SHOP, BAG & ITEMS, GARAGE, MORE) and MORE holds CAREER, MISSIONS, CREW, TOP, ESTATE, BANK, MESSAGES and SETTINGS; SHOP's shop list sets the GPS, BAG & ITEMS has its tabs, the messages row counts the unread. 6.12: MORE holds TASKS after CAREER (client/TasksApp.luau): the Tasks remote's state as three daily cards, the bonus card and the weekly card with their progress, rewards and the reset's countdown; CLAIM only on a done task (the gamepad lands on it) and CLAIM BONUS; the rewards waiting on TASKS, MORE and the HUD's phone dot; the toasts and sounds of a task done and a claim paid. 6.12: MORE holds STYLE after TASKS (client/StyleApp.luau, its 3D preview client/StylePreview.luau stubbed and recorded): the COURIER / VEHICLE tabs, a card an item with its price, BUY / WEAR / TAKE OFF through the Style remote, the reasons it can't (a reward, the level, the money), the purchase's sound, the keys (TAB, ↑ ↓, ENTER, L1 / R1) bound only while it is open. 6.12.5: home opens with the MAIN apps (JOBS across, MISSIONS and TASKS under it, red-edged, their live lines and bars: the chapter you are at or why it is shut, NEW on a chapter newly open, the dailies done and the rewards waiting), then six smaller tiles (MAP, SHOP, BAG & ITEMS, GARAGE, STYLE, MORE); MORE keeps CAREER, CREW, TOP, ESTATE, BANK, MESSAGES and SETTINGS; the rewards waiting badge TASKS, not MORE. 6.14: home opens with NEXT MISSION (client/ReadyCheck.luau, run for real with client/Gps.luau and client/SavingsLine.luau: the chapter and n/N, the mission, "0 of 2 ready" in red or "READY · START" in green, the savings goal's bar) over WORK (the JOBS app with its goal line), then TASKS, MISSIONS, MAP, SHOP, BAG & ITEMS, GARAGE, STYLE, MORE; NEXT MISSION opens the READY CHECK in the popup (the rows with ✔ / ✖ / ☆, a row's GPS to its shop, JOBS, INVITE through Roblox's game invite or else CREW, START through the MISSIONS window, OPEN MISSIONS, the savings goal kept live without a redraw for every coin); the one-time READY toast and its sting; the HUD's savings line under the cash growing the column, hidden through Marge's first day. 6.14 review: START only when it can work (READY · CALL CAR in amber with CALL CAR FIRST to the GARAGE, IN PROGRESS, LEADER PICKS in somebody else's crew, no READY toast for them), the savings line's money in its own label at the right and hidden when Hud says there is no room (SavingsRoom), the money without the level ("$1,800 SAVED · LEVEL 4", WORK and the result line too); the MISSIONS window counts the Roof Rack's slots for light pieces (the state's gear). 6.17: JOBS at a glance (client/JobsView.luau, shared/JobPick.luau): the hero card's choice (the best job you can take, a golden order first with its gold frame, ribbon, countdown, pay and TAKE GOLDEN ORDER; + ADD / + QUEUE during a run), the compact rows (the danger, the facts, the pay, "+", a lock's short words, a regular's medal, the open ones first), a row's or the hero's details (the old card, ‹ JOBS), the header's chips (YOUR RUN and NEXT UP as sub-views, rush hour's clock), ADD ALL's chip and toast, and on 320 × 448 to 1280 × 800 the hero beside the rows only in a wide popup, its height the popup's room and the chips in the header where they fit. A warning or failed check stops the command with a nonzero exit code.

This is a behavior check, not a Roblox renderer: fonts, automatic layout, touch hit testing, animation and actual device performance still require Studio. Production files are never rewritten by this tool. The regular required suite additionally runs the pure PhoneLayout regressions and compiles every module at Roblox's `-O0 -g2` settings.
