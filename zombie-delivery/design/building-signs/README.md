# Building signs: first art-direction preview

Status: owner approved the first preview on 2026-10-05 ("ou pěkny moc super"). All 54 individual signs are complete in [art/signs/](../../art/signs/README.md); branch `codex/building-signs` is ready for Claude review.

`first-preview.png` is an image-generated concept sheet, not an upload atlas or the final individual signs. It shows the five starting-area entries in `../buildings-brief.md`: depot, dealer, guns, mechanic and supplies.

Direction: custom die-cut silhouettes, chunky readable English names, cream/lavender/sage with business-specific teal and coral, dark plum outlines, restrained edge wear and original humorous mascots. The owner approved the similarly playful UI direction previously; the owner has approved this sign direction.

The final set has 40 wide 1024 × 256 boards and 14 square 512 × 512 badges. The names, motifs and gentle colors follow the approved sheet, with simplified vector mascots for distance readability. The final assets were drawn as individual SVGs, not cropped from this concept sheet.

[Browse the full gallery](../../art/signs/index.html), or inspect the seven category screenshots in [previews/](previews/). The PNG/SVG pairs, aliases, metadata, font attribution, rebuild instructions and integration notes are in [the asset handoff](../../art/signs/README.md).

No `World`, `Icons`, UI or gameplay modules are modified. Claude is implementing the UI in parallel. Registration/integration of final Roblox asset IDs remains Claude's task as described in the brief.
